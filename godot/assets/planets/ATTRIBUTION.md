# Planet Texture Attribution

The equirectangular planet textures in this directory (mercury, venus, earth,
mars, jupiter, saturn, uranus, neptune, luna, ceres) are from
[Solar System Scope](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), distributed
under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

- `earth.jpg`, `luna.jpg` (`8k_moon`), `mars.jpg`, `mercury.jpg`: the 8k maps; `jupiter.jpg`, `saturn.jpg`: 4k
- `venus.jpg` is the atmosphere map (`4k_venus_atmosphere`), desaturated to 0.55
- `mars.jpg` is desaturated to 0.62 and brightened by 1.12
- `ceres.jpg` is the artistic "fictional" map (`4k_ceres_fictional`)
- `earth_clouds.jpg` is `8k_earth_clouds` (greyscale)
- `saturn_ring.png` is `8k_saturn_ring_alpha` resampled to 2048 x 8
- `uranus.jpg`, `neptune.jpg`: 2k (no larger maps are published)
- all re-encoded as JPEG quality 90

Based on NASA elevation and imagery data.

## Moon textures (public domain)

- `europa.jpg`: Voyager/Galileo SSI global mosaic, 1024 x 512 (the south polar band, unmapped in the
  source, is filled with the nearest mapped row). Source: USGS Astrogeology, via
  [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Europa_Voyager_GalileoSSI_global_mosaic.jpg),
  public domain.
- `ganymede.jpg`: Voyager global map of Ganymede, 1440 x 720 (NASA/JPL/USGS), via
  [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ganymede_map_NASA_JPL_Voyager.jpg), public domain.
